Water Conservation

Simple habits that help your household and community save water.

Report leaks early

Report dripping taps, damaged pipes, and unusual water flow through Submit a Report.

Follow your water schedule

Prepare clean containers before distribution and collect only what your household needs.

Turn off unused taps

Close the tap while brushing your teeth or soaping your hands.

Store water carefully

Keep containers covered and use clean utensils when taking out stored water.

Reuse water thoughtfully

Use suitable leftover water for cleaning or watering non-edible plants when appropriate.

Share community updates

Help neighbors check official announcements and report changes in supply.
